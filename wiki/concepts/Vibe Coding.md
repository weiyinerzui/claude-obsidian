# Vibe Coding

A term describing the practice of building software through natural language conversation with AI coding agents rather than through manual, line-by-line programming. Popularized by creators who treat the AI as a co-founder rather than a subordinate tool.

## Core Characteristics

- **Natural language as interface**: Users describe problems, desired outcomes, and constraints in plain language
- **AI as co-founder**: The model is treated as a creative collaborator, not an employee executing orders
- **Iterative refinement**: Products evolve through conversation loops (describe → build → test → critique → refine)

## Historical Context

Before January 2025, AI coding tools were limited to demos and personal utilities. The watershed moment occurred when coding agents became capable of shipping production-ready products end-to-end to users beyond the creator themselves.

## The Shift

Vibe coding represents a fundamental redefinition of what it means to code:

- **Before AI**: Coding was a profession requiring years of formal training
- **After AI**: Coding is a general skill accessible to anyone with an idea and the ability to describe it

This mirrors the historical arc of writing: professional writers still exist, but writing itself is a universal skill that benefits everyone regardless of profession.

## Relationship to Engineering

Vibe coding does not replace software engineering. Engineering remains a serious discipline requiring training for production-critical systems. Vibe coding serves the vast space of personal tools, prototypes, and products that improve individual lives without requiring production-grade infrastructure.

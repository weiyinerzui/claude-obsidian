# Opinionated Products

A product philosophy holding that software succeeds not by doing everything for everyone, but by embodying a sharp, specific opinion that resonates with a targeted audience. In the AI era, where generic "all-in-one AI agent" products proliferate, the opinion behind the product is the primary differentiator.

## The Core Argument

> "Doing everything means it's doing nothing. Speaking to everybody means you're speaking to nobody."

Generic AI tools compete directly with general-purpose assistants like Claude or ChatGPT. If your product has no opinion, users may as well use the general tool. The product survives because its **opinion** resonates with people who share that worldview.

## Opinions Behind ljg-learn's Projects

| Product | Opinion |
|---------|---------|
| Front-End Slides | HTML is the next medium for storytelling; PowerPoint should be replaced |
| Codebase to Course | We should build first and learn later, reversing traditional CS education |
| Tab Out | Browsers only encourage opening tabs; we need to make closing tabs addictive |

## GitHub as Substack

In the past, having an opinion meant writing an article or making a video. Now, having an opinion can mean building a product or skill and publishing it on GitHub. Each product is an expression of how the creator thinks and their unique way of perceiving the world.

## Relationship to Scope

Opinionated products are naturally **small**. They target a specific use case for a specific audience. This is a feature, not a limitation: every big thing starts as a small thing with a sharp value proposition. Building small also aligns with building for fun and learning rather than for monetization.

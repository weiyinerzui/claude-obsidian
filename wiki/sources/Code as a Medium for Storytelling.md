# Code as a Medium for Storytelling

A highly reflective and practical presentation transcript by creator [[ljg-learn]] (who transitioned from a humanities/storytelling background into a prolific "vibe coder"). The talk maps out the philosophical shift of coding from a professional discipline to a generalized medium for personal self-expression, backed by the production of several successful open-source projects.

## Key Projects Discussed

1. **Front-End Slides**: An HTML-based presentation system replacing PowerPoint. Leverages the superior design capabilities of Claude Code, utilizing the [[Design MD]] and [[Playgrounds (LLM Design)]] concepts to prevent design slop.
2. **Tab Out Chrome Extension**: A browser productivity extension designed to make closing open tabs addictive. It groups tabs by domain and provides sensory feedback (sound effects, confetti) upon closing.
3. **YouTube Learning Tool**: An interactive learning companion that pulls transcripts, partitions videos into chapters, extracts quotes, and allows users to take audio-synchronized notes or remix transcripts into magazine articles or business biographies.
4. **Personal Ad Networks**: Habit-shaping interfaces, such as browser extensions that inject X (formerly Twitter) bookmarks into the top of the feed, or phone wallpapers displaying shuffling Japanese vocabulary cards.
5. **Content Remixing**: Projects like transforming the long-form *Acquired* podcast into a beautifully laid-out, printed biographic book or customized newsletters.
6. **Codebase to Course**: A tool that analyzes an existing GitHub repo or local codebase to auto-generate an interactive, layman-friendly computer science course.

## Core Philosophical Transitions

- **From Professional to General Skill**: Just as professional journalists and authors write, writing is fundamentally a general skill. In the AI era, coding is undergoing a similar shift. Professional engineering remains a specialized career, but coding is a tool accessible to everyone to improve their lives and express their taste.
- **Problem over Solution**: Developers should treat the AI as a co-founder rather than an assistant or employee. This involves describing the "mess" or problem rather than prescriptive technical specifications.
- **Opinionated vs. Generic**: Generic, all-in-one AI products act like "doing everything means doing nothing." To stand out, products must be small, highly targeted, and run on a strong opinion or "soul" (e.g., [[Build to Learn]], [[Vibe Coding]]).
- **Subtractive Product Management**: Because LLMs can trivially append features, the modern developer's role is primarily subtractive — cutting unnecessary bloat to align with empirical, real-world habits.
